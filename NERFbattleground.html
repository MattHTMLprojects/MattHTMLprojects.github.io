Nerf Battleground

Everyone starts unarmed. Find a loot box to obtain a blaster and darts. One dart eliminates a player. Last player standing wins.

Your vision is limited to a flashlight-style field of view. Bunkers, rocks, and trees block your flashlight beam.

At two minutes remaining, bots become more aggressive and gain smarter navigation and combat strafing.

At one minute remaining, surviving bots can fly over any object and pursue their targets directly.
