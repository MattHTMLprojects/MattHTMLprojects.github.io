RETRO DRIVE 2020

A clean-room, single-file DOS-style road challenge.

W / UP accelerate   S / DOWN brake
A D / LEFT RIGHT steer   P / ESC pause
R restart   F fullscreen

Xbox: left stick steers, RT accelerates, LT brakes, Menu pauses, View restarts.

Reach the finish before 2:45. Stay on the road and avoid traffic.

Controller support uses the browser Gamepad API. Press any controller button after connecting.
