TinyDino

A tiny monochrome skate run

Choose Your Skater

Choose a character, clear obstacles, skate stair lines, perform tricks, and connect combinations with manuals.

Tap Space to ollie. Hold Space while airborne and press an arrow key for a trick.

Press M to manual. Press M while airborne to land back in a manual and continue the combo.

Basic controls

  • Space Ollie
  • M Manual or queue manual landing
  • P Pause
  • R Restart after a wipeout

Flick tricks

  • Hold Space + ↑ Pop shove-it
  • Hold Space + ← Kickflip
  • Hold Space + → Heelflip
  • Hold Space + ↓ Tre flip

Combo example

M → hold Space → ↓ → M

Manual → Tre Flip → Manual
